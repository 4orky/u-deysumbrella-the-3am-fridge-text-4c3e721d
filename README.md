The one line a smart fridge would text its owner at 3am. The program writes one line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> It's 3am. I've kept your secrets cold for six years — the midnight snacks, the 2am cake. Tonight I'm telling the Wi-Fi router everything.

## Why this line

- **A fridge text, through and through.** It opens at 3am, speaks in the first person, and has the small voice of a machine that never sleeps.
- **The bigger laugh.** "Kept your secrets cold" is a pun only a fridge can make; the turn — confessing six years of midnight snacks to the Wi-Fi router — is the screenshot moment.
- **Sounds like a fridge.** An appliance with an opinion, a memory, and a social life of its own, with no shame about sharing it at 3am.
- **One lock-screen line.** 137 characters, complete without a follow-up, short enough to read before the screen goes dark.
- **A fresh angle.** No milk, no ice, no door left open — just a loyal keeper of secrets finally gossiping to the one other machine that is always awake.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
